    Project Management — Scrum

    Scrum is an agile framework that divides projects into iterative 1–4-week sprints, each aimed at delivering a potentially shippable product increment. The Product Owner prioritizes the Product Backlog, the Scrum Master facilitates the process, and the Development Team collaborates on the selected work.

    • Daily Scrums keep the team aligned
    • Sprint Planning sets goals and selects tasks
    • Sprint Review showcases the increment and gathers feedback
    • Sprint Retrospective drives continuous improvement
